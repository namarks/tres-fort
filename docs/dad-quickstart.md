# Getting Started with Très Fort 💪

Hey Dad — here's everything you need to get going. Takes about 10 minutes to
set up once, then it's dead simple day to day.

---

## The big idea (read this first)

Très Fort is **two things working together**:

1. **The app on your phone** is your gym logbook. It always knows *today's
   workout* — you just open it, do the exercises, and tap to record each set.
   A rest timer runs between sets. That's it.

2. **Claude is your coach.** This is the part that's different from every other
   fitness app: your actual training plan — what lifts, how heavy, how it
   changes week to week — gets built and adjusted by **chatting with Claude**,
   not by tapping around in the app. Think of Claude as a coach you text.

So: **you do the workout in the app, you talk to the coach in Claude.** The two
stay in sync automatically.

---

## One-time setup

**1. Sign in.** Open the app, tap *Sign in with Apple*. Done — you have an
account.

**2. Join Nick's group.** Nick will text you a 6-character code (looks like
`K7M4PQ`). In the app, go to the **Group** tab and enter it. Now you two can
see each other's workouts and Nick can cheer you on.

**3. Connect your rides** *(do this if you ride or run with a watch).* In the
app's setup, tap **Connect with intervals.icu** and log in. This lets your
coach see your cardio and balance it against your lifting. If your watch isn't
feeding intervals.icu yet, Nick will help you hook that up first — it's the one
fiddly part, and it's worth getting right.

**4. Connect your coach.** In Très Fort, tap **Set up my coach** on Today,
or open coach setup from **Profile**. Choose **Claude**, then tap **Connect
with Claude**. Sign in to Claude and confirm the prefilled Très Fort connector.
Choose **Open Très Fort to review access**, review what Claude can access,
and allow the connection. Return to Claude when prompted. If the link opens
Claude without the setup form, return to setup and press and hold **Connect
with Claude** to copy the link, then paste it into Safari.

If Claude asks for a code, return to coach setup, expand **Use a connect
code**, tap **Generate connect code**, and copy it. Paste it only on the
Très Fort consent page, never into a chat. Claude account availability and
usage limits depend on your plan.

**Prefer Codex?** Choose **Codex** in the same setup screen, tap **Copy setup
for Codex**, and paste it into Codex. Initial setup currently needs the
computer running Codex. After pairing ChatGPT Remote, you can use that
connection from your phone while the computer stays online.

That's it. Your coach can now see your training.

## Your daily routine

- **Workout day?** Open the app → today's exercises are already there → do them,
  tapping in your weight and reps as you go. The rest timer handles itself.
- **Want to change something?** ("My shoulder's sore," "can we go lighter
  today," "I want to add more leg work) — just **tell Claude.** Your plan
  updates and shows up in the app.
- **Curious how you're doing?** Ask Claude: *"How's my bench trending?"* or
  *"Am I getting stronger?"*

---

## Things to say to your coach (Claude) to get started

Once Très Fort is connected, just open a Claude chat and try:

> *"I'm new to Très Fort. I'm 65, I want to get stronger and stay healthy, I can
> lift 3 days a week, and I ride my bike on weekends. Build me a starting plan."*

> *"What's my workout today?"*

> *"I felt great today, that was too easy — bump it up next time."*

Claude does the rest — writes the plan, sets your weights, and adapts it as you
go. The app just shows you what to do.
