**Draft dated 2026-09-12. Not published or in effect.** Prepared for owner and
legal review before worldwide availability in provider-supported countries.
The public version needs an effective date, a published URL, and an acceptance
flow appropriate to the app and plugin. This document does not attest that the
service is eligible to operate in every country.

Très Fort app, website and account connections. Contact
[nick@tresfort.app](mailto:nick@tresfort.app) with questions or concerns.

## The service

Très Fort helps you organize training plans, record workouts and review your
training history. You can choose to connect supported AI apps and other
services. Features and provider compatibility may change. We do not promise
particular training results or uninterrupted availability.

Très Fort is a training tool, not a medical service. Training recommendations,
including AI-generated recommendations, can be incomplete or unsuitable for
you. Review suggested changes before following them and seek appropriate
professional advice about medical conditions, injuries or exercise suitability.

## Your account and choices

Use your own account and keep your sign-in and connection credentials private.
Provide information you are entitled to use. You can review and change training
records through supported app features, disconnect integrations, export account
data and request account deletion in the app.

Très Fort is not directed to children under 13. If you are under the age at which
you can independently agree to these terms where you live, use requires a
parent or guardian's permission and any consent required by applicable law.
Connected providers may have higher minimum ages and their own eligibility
requirements.

## AI apps and other providers

Connecting an AI app permits it, and the model provider you use through it, to
request the training information described on the approval screen and to make
authorized training changes. This can include imported Apple Health and
Intervals.icu workouts and group information available to your account. Choose
apps and providers you trust and review their privacy policies.

Coaching conversations take place in the AI app you choose. You are responsible
for obtaining access to that app under its terms. The free Très Fort connection
does not include model API usage, an AI subscription or a guarantee that a
provider will support your account. Any provider fees are charged by that
provider. Très Fort does not collect your model API keys for this connection.

Disconnecting stops future access through that Très Fort connection. It does
not erase information already retrieved into an AI conversation or another
provider. Manage those copies with the relevant provider. Très Fort's Apple
Health group-sharing setting does not limit your own authorized coach's access.

## Your data and content

You retain your rights in the information you provide. You permit Très Fort to
store, process and display it as needed to provide the features you choose,
including sharing with groups or integrations you authorize. This permission
is limited to operating the service as described in the
[privacy policy](https://tresfort.app/privacy).

Deleting a connection and deleting an account have different effects. The
privacy policy describes retention, deletion and copies outside Très Fort.
These terms do not replace the separate choices and disclosures shown when
you authorize data access.

## Respectful and lawful use

Do not use Très Fort to harass others, share material you have no right to
share, access someone else's account, bypass access controls or deliberately
disrupt the service. Group settings provide ways to leave, report concerns and
block another member. We may restrict access when reasonably necessary to
address abuse, protect users or meet legal obligations. Where appropriate and
permitted, we will explain the restriction and how to contact us about it.

## Changes and ending use

You may stop using the service at any time. You can export supported account
data and use the app's account-deletion process. If a material service change
or discontinuation affects access to your data, we will give reasonable notice
and an opportunity to retrieve it where reasonably possible, except when urgent
security or legal obligations prevent this.

We will identify material changes to these terms and provide notice before
they take effect when required. Changes will not remove rights that already
apply to you. A future paid offering would have its own clearly disclosed
price and terms; these terms do not authorize recurring charges.

## Your legal rights

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits rights or remedies that cannot
lawfully be excluded or limited. Applicable consumer protections continue to
apply. These terms do not require arbitration, waive class actions, impose an
exclusive foreign court or set a monetary limit on liability.

Confirm the operator/contact details, eligibility/age approach, notice and
